Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026
Sold by Paddle. Qrynt subscription payments are processed by Paddle.com, our authorised reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle handles all billing, invoicing, tax collection, and payment disputes on our behalf.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your Qrynt subscription, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase date, no questions asked.

This applies to first-time purchases of Pro and Team plans. Renewal charges are eligible for a refund if requested within 7 days of the renewal date.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us by email:

  • Email qrynttech@gmail.com with the subject line Refund Request
  • Include your registered email address and the date of purchase
  • We will process your request within 2 business days

Alternatively, you can contact Paddle directly at paddle.com/legal/customer-support for billing-related issues.

Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, it is processed by Paddle and typically appears on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Requests made more than 30 days after the initial purchase
  • Renewal charges requested more than 7 days after the renewal date
  • Accounts that have been suspended for Terms of Service violations

Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard → Billing → Manage subscription. Cancellation stops future charges. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund — submit a refund request separately if eligible.

Disputes and Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us before initiating a chargeback. Paddle, as Merchant of Record, will respond to any dispute filed with your bank. Unresolved disputes may result in account suspension.
