Common questions

Is my data stored when I process documents?
No. By default Qrynt processes all documents in memory only — nothing is written to disk or retained after your session ends. Storage is an opt-in feature on Pro and Team tiers, with user-controlled retention and deletion.
Can I run Qrynt on my own infrastructure?
Yes. Enterprise plans include a Docker image for self-hosted deployment. Your data never leaves your environment. Contact us to get started.
What counts as a request?
One call to /ingest followed by /resolve counts as one request. Calling /export multiple times on the same resolved session does not count as additional requests.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, any time from your dashboard. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period — no partial refunds. Cancelling moves you to the Starter tier automatically.
Do you offer discounts for startups or students?
Yes. Email us with proof of startup or student status and we will apply a discount manually.
